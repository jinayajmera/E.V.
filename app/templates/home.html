Here's what we offer.

one

One person, one vote

Each voter is tied to a single identity, e-mail, phone number and photograph. Duplicate and look-alike accounts are stopped at registration.

two

Private & protected

Strong password protection, automatic lock-out after repeated failures and encrypted sessions keep every account safe.

three

Open, auditable results

Every action is recorded. Results are public once the election closes and always add up to the votes cast.
